LandwiseAI — System Architecture

AI-powered Tamil Nadu land-title verification. Legal advisors organise land into Projects → Parcels, upload Encumbrance Certificates & Sale Deeds; the platform extracts, matches, cross-validates, scores and reports on the title chain with Google Gemini — over a bilingual Tamil/English reviewer cockpit.
